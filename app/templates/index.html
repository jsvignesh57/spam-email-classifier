How the Classification Pipeline Works

1

Text Normalization

The email is processed by src/preprocess.py: header prefixes are removed, emails, URLs, and numbers are replaced with semantic tokens, and whitespace is normalized.

2

TF-IDF Vectorization

The normalized text is transformed into a 121,288-dimensional word-level TF-IDF feature vector (unigrams + bigrams) with sublinear term-frequency scaling.

3

LinearSVC Margin

The frozen Linear Support Vector Classifier calculates the signed geometric distance between the email's feature vector and the decision hyperplane.

4

Verdict Output

If the decision score is ≥ 0.0, the email is classified as SPAM; otherwise, it is returned as NOT SPAM.
